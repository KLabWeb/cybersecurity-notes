# Cybersecurity Notes

Study notes taken while working through my structured [application security study plan](https://github.com/KLabWeb/cybersecurity-study-plan) — my transition from full-stack web development into application security (AppSec) engineering.

Notes are organized by phase and topic and grow as I move through the plan. They also serve as the raw material for my interview-prep note cards and for sharing with others wishing to learn more on cyb or transition to cyb themselves.

## Why Such Lengthy Notes?
All of these notes are notes I took myself, in my own words. That is much of the point, really; to read the theory and see examples in practice, then put this knowledge into my own words and code snippets. Through this, I ensure I fully understand the concept, plus help concrete it in my memory.

Additionally, I often reference my notes at later times and have dozens of 35+ page notes files, both digital copies and paper print-outs, that I can use to refresh any once learned topic again quickly. No need to read a 542 page book again to get a full picture. Just read the notes.

I also use these notes as the basis for notecards, which can be used for even faster refreshing of knowledge and memorization. For software engineering categories, I have probably over 750 note-cards combined, everything from JavaScript, to Domain Driven Design, to AJAX, to SQL & Database Normalization, to Docker. 

Yes, time to take the notes, the note cards, but in doing so (in combo with PLENTY of project work too, of course), concrete, easy to remember, and well grasped foundations, concepts, and details.
